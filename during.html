Navigating Your Engaged Learning Experience

Staying on Track Once the Work Begins

Once your project is underway, the challenge shifts from preparation to execution. Plans rarely unfold exactly as expected, and part of engaged learning is developing the skills to adapt, communicate, and keep your team and partner aligned as the work evolves.

Managing the Project

Keep Communication Consistent

Regular check-ins with your partner and your team prevent small misunderstandings from becoming larger problems. Set a recurring meeting cadence early and stick to it, even when there isn't a major update to share.

Track Progress and Roadblocks

Use a shared document or project board to record what's been completed, what's in progress, and what's blocked. This keeps the whole team accountable and gives your partner visibility into how things are going.

Practice Ethical Research and Design

If your project involves interviews, user testing, or working with sensitive community data, revisit your ethical commitments regularly. Ask for consent clearly, protect people's information, and be transparent about how their input will be used.

When Things Don't Go as Planned

Scope changes, unresponsive partners, and shifting priorities are normal parts of real-world projects. Document changes as they happen, communicate them to your team and instructor, and revisit your original goals to see what still applies.
