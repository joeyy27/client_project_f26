Wrapping Up and Reflecting on Your Experience

Closing Out with Purpose

How you end a project matters as much as how you start it. A responsible closeout protects the relationship with your partner and sets up whoever continues the work — including future students — for success.

Finishing the Work

Hand Off Cleanly

Organize your files, documentation, and any code or design assets so your partner (or the next student team) can pick up where you left off without confusion. Include a summary of decisions you made and why.

Close the Loop with Your Partner

Share a final update or presentation with your partner that covers what was accomplished, what's left unfinished, and any recommendations for next steps.

Reflect on Your Growth

Look back at the learning goals you set before the project started. Which ones did you meet? What surprised you? Reflection turns experience into something you can actually articulate later.

Turning Experience into Evidence

The skills and stories from this project don't end when the semester does. Translate what you did into resume bullet points, portfolio pieces, and interview talking points that show real, applied impact.
